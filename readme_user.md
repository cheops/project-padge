# Your Fox Badge — Quick Guide

## What it does

Your badge lights up on its own when it senses motion, and it can also be
triggered with a quick button press. It sleeps the rest of the time to save
battery.

## The button

There's one hidden button. Here's what it does:

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Quick press** | Plays the next light animation |
| **Press and hold (~2 seconds)** | Switches mode (see below) — flashes green or red to confirm |

There are 7 different animations (ripples, sparkle, heartbeat, blinking eyes,
smile, wink, and more) — each quick press moves to the next one.

## The two modes

- 🟢 **Motion mode** (default) — the badge lights up whenever it's moved,
  shaken, or bumped, *and* also on a quick button press.
- 🔴 **Manual mode** — the badge stays quiet until you press the button. Use
  this if you want it to only light up on command, or to save battery when
  it's not being worn.

Hold the button for ~2 seconds to switch between the two. A green flash means
you just switched into Motion mode; a red flash means Manual mode.

## Battery

- Powered by two CR2032 coin cell batteries.
- The badge sleeps almost all the time and only wakes briefly to animate, so
  batteries should last a good while under normal use.
- If the lights start looking dim or don't turn on at all, it's time to
  swap in fresh batteries.
- 🔵 If the badge repeatedly flashes blue instead of playing an animation,
  that's an internal error signal — it means something needs attention.
  It'll still work as a plain light-up badge from the button, but please get
  in touch so we can help.

## Tips

- No need to turn it "off" — Manual mode plus not touching the button is
  effectively off, and it uses almost no power while asleep.
- The badge is dim by design (to protect the small batteries), so it's meant
  to be seen up close or in low light rather than in bright daylight.
